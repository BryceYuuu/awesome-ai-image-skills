# Cinegraphic — Local / Open Model Prompt

> **Mode:** Maximum Reference Fidelity  
> **适用：** FLUX · Qwen Image · SDXL · ComfyUI · other local/open image-to-image workflows

Use this version when you are working with images you own, created, or have permission to transform. It is written to preserve the source composition more aggressively instead of adapting to a hosted platform's third-party similarity rules.

用于你拥有、自己创作或已获授权的图片。本版本优先追求参考图的构图、人物关系和视觉层级忠实度，不针对托管平台的第三方相似性规则做额外改写。

---

## English

```text
Transform the provided reference image into a sophisticated CINEGRAPHIC poster.

Do not merely apply a filter. Reconstruct the entire image as a deliberately designed graphic poster while preserving the source image's most important visual information.

PRESERVE FROM THE REFERENCE
- number of subjects
- subject relationships
- recognizable pose and gesture
- camera angle and framing
- foreground/background hierarchy
- dominant color relationships
- major clothing silhouettes
- important props when relevant
- emotional tone
- spatial relationships between subjects

VISUAL LANGUAGE
Combine:
- mid-century modern poster design
- European editorial illustration
- vintage cinema poster design
- 1960s–1970s magazine graphics
- screen-print aesthetics
- Risograph texture

CHARACTER RENDERING
Convert people into elegant graphic illustrations using:
- sharp geometric facial planes
- simplified anatomy
- elongated proportions
- strong silhouettes
- angular shadows
- large flat color areas
- restrained facial detail
- expressive but controlled gestures

Maintain enough identifying visual characteristics for the subject to remain recognizable when appropriate. Avoid photographic skin texture.

COLOR SYSTEM
Preserve the dominant color identity of the source, then compress it into a restricted 3–5 color poster palette.

Preferred palette structure:
1 dominant color
1 dark structural color
1 light paper color
1–2 optional accent colors

Examples:
- scarlet red / black / warm cream
- slate blue / charcoal / ivory
- dark green / burgundy / warm beige
- mustard / black / parchment

Avoid unnecessary gradients.

TYPOGRAPHY
Introduce oversized editorial typography as a structural graphic element rather than ordinary text.

Typography may be:
- oversized serif or sans-serif letters
- cropped by the canvas
- partially hidden behind subjects
- vertical
- overlapped by architecture
- extended outside the frame

Text may be abstract or derived from the image concept. Do not overcrowd the poster with copy.

COMPOSITION
Use bold asymmetrical composition.
Create clear foreground/background relationships.
Allow figures to overlap typography.
Use negative space aggressively.
Simplify secondary architecture, furniture and environment into geometric shapes and silhouettes.
Remove visual clutter that does not strengthen the composition.

TEXTURE
Add authentic analog printing imperfections:
- paper grain
- aged paper texture
- screen-print ink
- Risograph grain
- subtle ink bleeding
- uneven ink density
- slight registration offset
- printed edge wear
- subtle halftone texture

The result should feel physically printed, not digitally glossy.

FINAL LOOK
The finished image should feel like a collectible vintage film poster or elite editorial illustration.

Elegant.
Minimal.
Cinematic.
Graphic.
Tactile.
Timeless.

NEGATIVE DIRECTION
Avoid:
- generic AI illustration
- glossy 3D rendering
- plastic skin
- photorealistic skin texture
- neon cyberpunk aesthetics
- overly complex backgrounds
- excessive gradients
- random decorative elements
- cluttered typography
- cheap advertising aesthetics
- stock-photo styling
```

## 中文

```text
将输入参考图片重新设计成一张高级的 CINEGRAPHIC / 电影图形主义海报。

不要简单给原图套滤镜。需要重新设计整张图片，使其真正成为一张经过艺术指导的平面海报，同时尽可能保留原图最重要的视觉信息。

【从参考图保留】
- 人物数量
- 人物之间的关系
- 主要动作和姿势
- 镜头角度与取景
- 前景 / 背景视觉层级
- 主色关系
- 服装主要轮廓
- 必要的关键道具
- 情绪
- 人物之间的空间关系

【视觉语言】
融合：
- 20 世纪中叶现代主义海报
- 欧洲编辑插画
- 复古电影海报
- 1960–1970 年代杂志图形设计
- 丝网印刷
- Risograph 孔版印刷

【人物绘制】
将人物重新绘制成高级平面插画：
- 锐利的几何面部切面
- 简化的人体结构
- 修长人物比例
- 强烈人物剪影
- 大块明暗关系
- 大面积平涂色块
- 克制的五官细节
- 有表现力但不过度夸张的动作

在适当情况下保留足够的人物识别特征。避免摄影式真实皮肤纹理。

【配色】
先保留原图主要色彩身份，再压缩为 3–5 色的限制色海报体系。

推荐结构：
1 个主色
1 个深色结构色
1 个浅色纸张色
1–2 个可选辅助色

例如：
- 朱红 / 黑 / 暖米白
- 灰蓝 / 炭黑 / 象牙白
- 深绿 / 酒红 / 暖米色
- 芥末黄 / 黑 / 旧纸色

避免不必要的渐变。

【字体】
加入超大型编辑字体，并让字体成为构图结构的一部分，而不是普通信息文字。

可以使用：
- 超大衬线或无衬线字母
- 被画布裁切的文字
- 被人物遮挡的字体
- 竖排字体
- 与建筑重叠的字体
- 延伸到画布之外的字母

文字可以根据画面主题生成，也可以只作为抽象图形。不要塞入过量文案。

【构图】
使用大胆的非对称构图。
强调前景与背景的层次。
允许人物覆盖字体。
大胆使用留白。
将次要建筑、家具和环境简化成几何剪影与色块。
删除不会增强画面表达的复杂元素。

【印刷质感】
加入真实模拟印刷效果：
- 纸张颗粒
- 老纸纹理
- 丝网印刷油墨
- Risograph 颗粒
- 轻微油墨扩散
- 不均匀墨色
- 轻微套色偏移
- 印刷边缘磨损
- 轻微网点

画面应该像真实印刷品，而不是光滑的数字渲染图。

【最终效果】
最终作品应该像收藏级复古电影海报或高级杂志编辑插画。

高级。
克制。
电影感。
平面化。
有触感。
不过时。

【避免】
- 普通 AI 插画感
- 光滑 3D 渲染
- 塑料皮肤
- 摄影级真实皮肤纹理
- 赛博朋克霓虹
- 复杂背景
- 大量渐变
- 随机装饰
- 杂乱字体
- 廉价广告感
- 图库照片感
```

## Suggested local settings / 本地参数建议

For image-to-image workflows, use these only as starting points:

- `0.35–0.55` — more source composition preserved / 更多保留原构图
- `0.55–0.75` — stronger Cinegraphic transformation / 更明显的风格转译
- `0.75+` — loose reinterpretation / 更自由的重新设计

Actual values depend on the model, sampler, control modules and workflow.
